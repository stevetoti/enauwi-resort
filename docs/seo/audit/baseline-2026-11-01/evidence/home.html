Your Island Retreat

Where the Ocean Meets Island Soul

E'Nauwi Beach Resort is a family-friendly island retreat set along a peaceful beachfront with calm lagoon waters and beautiful island views. The resort offers a relaxed environment ideal for families, couples, and groups seeking comfort, good food, and genuine island hospitality.

Slow Down. Dive In. Feel at Home.

Spend your mornings floating in a lagoon so still it mirrors the sky. Cool off in our pool beneath swaying coconut palms. Paddle a kayak to one of four pristine sandy islands for an afternoon of snorkelling. Then settle into the restaurant as the sun dips behind the islands and the aroma of freshly grilled seafood fills the evening air.

Travelling with little ones? They'll love the jumping castle and trampoline while you enjoy a quiet moment — our nanny service runs 8 am to 8 pm so you can truly relax. Whether it's a family holiday, a romantic escape, or a group getaway, E'Nauwi wraps every guest in the kind of warmth you only find on a Melanesian island.

Beachfront Rooms & Garden Bungalows

Wake to the whisper of waves in a lagoon-front bungalow, or retreat to a garden hideaway wrapped in frangipani and birdsong. Every room is designed so the island comes to you.

Kayaking & Snorkeling

Glide across the glass-calm lagoon by kayak and explore four beautiful sandy beach islands just minutes from the resort. Slip beneath the surface to discover coral gardens alive with tropical fish, sea turtles, and our resident dugongs — three gentle giants who call our lagoon home.

Open-Air Restaurant & Bar

Dine under the palms with the lagoon stretching out before you. Fresh island seafood, tropical flavours, and a well-stocked bar — all set to the gentle rhythm of live background music.

Getting Here

Your Journey to Paradise

Getting to E'Nauwi Beach Resort is part of the adventure. Located on the stunning island of Efate, home to Vanuatu's capital Port Vila.

1

Fly to Port Vila (VLI)

International and domestic flights arrive at Bauerfield International Airport in Port Vila, the capital of Vanuatu on Efate Island.

Direct flights from Australia & NZ
2

Scenic Drive to the Resort

Our shuttle service can pick you up from the airport — enjoy the scenic coastal drive through lush tropical countryside. Contact us 72 hours prior to arrange pick-up.

VUV 2,000/adult · VUV 1,000/child
3

Arrive at E'Nauwi Beach Resort

Be greeted with a refreshing welcome drink and a warm Melanesian 'welkam' as you step into paradise. Check-in is seamless and stress-free.

Welcome drink included
