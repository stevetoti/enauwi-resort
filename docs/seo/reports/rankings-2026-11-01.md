## Business implications

- Brand query "enauwi beach resort": home page #1 in Vanuatu and Australia; New Zealand #5 with the Port Vila directions article. NZ brand consistency and internal links need attention.
- Australia: "corporate retreat vanuatu" #5 with the conference article; "kayaking efate" #10 with /blog. Confirm active conference facilities before promotion.
- Accommodation opportunity: "family accommodation efate" #25 in Vanuatu; "beachfront bungalows efate" #31 in Australia. Both currently use the home page rather than the intended booking page.
- Activities: "snorkeling efate" uses /activities at #32 VU and #47 AU. The specific snorkelling page is the intended owner. NZ "family activities efate" #22 uses /activities.
- No controlled ranking improvement is inferred. Repeat the same country/device/language/query checks after recrawling and indexing, with each snapshot retained. No recurring monitor was created.

## Limits and provenance

- Five checks still returned provider search-engine errors after one retry. Full error responses and first attempts are retained. Do not include them as rank misses in success-rate calculations.
- Requested depth is not the actual organic count. Google features, result availability and provider response determine the returned coverage. A domain not observed in 20 returned results is not proven absent from the top 100.
- Provider historical keyword data returned no ranked-keyword records for the domain in AU/NZ. Live checks above are direct observations and take precedence for this baseline; the historical result is not evidence of zero rankings.
- AU/NZ demand metrics are estimated search volumes, not resort sessions or leads. VU Labs keyword volume is unsupported. Missing data remains null.
- Source records: research/measured-2026-11-01/rankings.json and serp-{country}-{query-number}.json. Full 90-row export: ../rankings.csv. Methodology: https://docs.dataforseo.com/v3/serp-se-type-live-advanced/.
